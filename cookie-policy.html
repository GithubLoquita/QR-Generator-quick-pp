Cookie & Storage Policy

This policy explains how QR Studio utilizes browser storage mechanisms (including Cookies, LocalStorage, and SessionStorage) to deliver a seamless, privacy-preserving experience.

What Browser Storage Is Used?

Unlike commercial platforms that place persistent cross-site tracking cookies on your device, QR Studio uses lightweight client-side storage purely for necessary functionality and minimal metrics:

Storage Key / Type Category Duration Purpose & Contents
qr_studio_terms_consent Essential (Local) Persistent Records your acknowledgment of the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy draft version so you are not prompted repeatedly.
qr_studio_file_upload_consent Essential (Local) Persistent Records your confirmation that you hold lawful rights to process your selected image and PDF files.
Third-Party Tracking Cookies None N/A None. Zero cookies are set by QR Studio. All telemetry and external counting endpoints have been removed.

Future Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

QR Studio is currently 100% free with zero third-party advertisement banners. However, in preparation for potential future Google AdSense integration, we transparently disclose the following:

  • Third-Party Vendors: Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.
  • DoubleClick Cookie: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Opt-Out Options: You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • Consent Management: Before any advertising cookies are activated, a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) or compliant consent modal will be activated to allow granular user choice.

How to Clear or Block Storage

You can remove all stored keys from QR Studio at any time directly through your browser settings:

  • Chrome / Edge: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies / Site data → Search for "qrenerator.vercel.app" → Clear data.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data.
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data → Remove.
